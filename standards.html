Euro Paper (AJP) Ltd

Made for daily life.
Built for business.

EURO PAPER (AJP) LTD is a UK-based manufacturer specialising in household and away-from-home sanitary paper products. From Blackburn, we deliver reliable paper solutions to businesses across the UK and beyond.

Established in 2019, the company operates from India Mill in Blackburn, England. Industry expertise, efficient production and a customer-focused approach come together to meet the growing demand for hygienic, everyday paper essentials.

We focus on consistent standards, dependable service and long-term relationships with customers and suppliers. Through quality, operational excellence and continuous growth, EURO PAPER (AJP) LTD supports businesses with trusted, cost-effective solutions.

Accreditation marks

Clarity matters
in every claim.

EuroPapers materials include the CHSA Accredited Manufacturer of Soft Tissue mark and FSC materials. These marks belong in a quiet, factual part of the experience—not as decoration or a broad promise.

01

Product-specific information

FSC status and any related declaration should be confirmed for the product and order in question.

02

Clear specification

Product matrices give the code, ply, pack format, dimensions, case and pallet quantities, and barcode.

03

Responsible communication

We keep certification marks accurate, legible and secondary to the product information they support.
