Euro Paper (AJP) Ltd

Made for daily life.
Built for business.

EURO PAPER (AJP) LTD is a UK-based manufacturer specialising in household and away-from-home hygiene paper products. From Blackburn, we deliver reliable paper solutions to businesses across the UK and beyond.

Established in 2019, the company operates from India Mill in Blackburn, England. Industry expertise, efficient production and a customer-focused approach come together to meet the growing demand for hygienic, everyday paper essentials.

We focus on consistent standards, dependable service and long-term relationships with customers and suppliers. Through quality, operational excellence and continuous growth, EURO PAPER (AJP) LTD supports businesses with trusted, cost-effective solutions.
